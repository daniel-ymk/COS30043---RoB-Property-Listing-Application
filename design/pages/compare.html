Compare homes

Up to 4 listings side by side. The best value in each row is marked green.

Dev notes · Compare (/compare) · Jason
  • IDs live in a Pinia store (persisted to localStorage) and in the URL: /compare?ids=1,3,10. Data: properties.php?ids=1,3,10.
  • "Compare" checkbox on every PropertyCard adds or removes; a 5th shows a toast "You can compare up to 4 properties".
  • Table scrolls sideways on mobile, with the row labels sticky on the left.
  • Green = lowest price, lowest price per m², most bedrooms / bathrooms / parking / area. Prices are only ranked when every listing is rent, or every listing is sale.
