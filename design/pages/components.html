Rules
  • Headings: Lexend. Everything else: Source Sans 3.
  • Body never under 16px; small text never under 14px.
  • Prices always Lexend 600, with "/month" for rent.
  • Sentence case everywhere. Buttons say verb + object: "Book viewing", not "Submit".
