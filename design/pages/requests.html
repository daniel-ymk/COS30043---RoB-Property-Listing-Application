Viewing requests

Confirm or decline requests on your listings. Declining frees the day for someone else.

    Dev notes · Viewing requests (/owner/requests) · Khant
    • GET appointments.php?as=owner&status=pending (tab = status). Navbar badge = meta.total of pending.
    • Confirm / decline: PATCH appointments.php?id= {action}. Move the card out of the Pending tab with a toast. Wrong status → 409: reload the list.
    • Oldest viewing date first, so the soonest viewings are at the top.
